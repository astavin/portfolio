About Me

It was just a dream...

My name is Ala Iurco. I was born and grew up in Moldova, a small Eastern European country between Romania and Ukraine.

About 10 years ago, I moved to the United States to start a new chapter in my life.

My journey has been full of growth and determination. I focused on learning English, building my education, and creating a stable career.

Each step motivates me to continue growing, improving my skills, and moving forward with determination toward my future goals.

Life is not always easy, and I have faced personal challenges along the way. These experiences strengthened my resilience and motivated me to continue growing both personally and professionally.
